Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Every project is different. Here are a few things we can clarify before getting started.

What should I share in my first inquiry?

A short overview of your business, the service you need, your target audience and any preferred timeline. If you have an existing website or brand reference, share that too.

Can you refresh an existing website or identity?

Yes. Share what is working and what you want to improve. We can discuss a focused refresh or a broader redesign after reviewing your requirements.

Can you help if we are starting a brand from scratch?

Yes. We can discuss your positioning, visual identity, website and marketing needs and propose a suitable starting point.

Will my website work on mobile and desktop?

Responsive design can be included in your website scope, with layouts planned for phones, tablets, laptops and larger displays.

Can we request only one service?

Absolutely. You can enquire about one service or discuss a combination of branding, marketing, web and app requirements.

How do pricing and timelines work?

They depend on features, content, complexity and deliverables. Share a brief overview and we can discuss a tailored scope and estimate.

What happens after launch?

We can discuss handover, future updates and ongoing support as part of your project scope. Hosting, maintenance and campaign management should be agreed separately in the proposal.
